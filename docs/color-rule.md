# Green palette rule (derived from ep01 v7)
Green share = green px (G-B>35, G>=R) / non-black px (max ch >30), frames at 80% of each scene.

ep01 v7 per scene:
- s1 hook: green = price "Rp10rb" only; bowl, glow, labels white. 2.0%
- s2 setup: no green (bowl + coin + label white). 0.0%
- s3 number: green = salary figure + "268 mangkok" counter (same quantity); ring, dots, labels white. 9.9%
- s4 compare: green = UMP figure + "572 mangkok" counter; ring/dots white. 7.9%
- s5 punchline: green = the 268 counted dots + "bojonegoro 268" label (the counted unit); rest white. 29.3%
- s6 CTA: no green. 0.0%

Rule:
1. Green = ONE focus idea per screen: the money figure or the counted unit (and its counter, which is the same quantity).
2. White = structure: rings, tracks, outlines, objects, labels, captions (caption key words are SemiBold white, never green).
3. Two green items may coexist only if they express the same quantity (number + its counter). Never two different ideas green at once.
4. Setup and CTA may have zero green.
5. Glow is white light on the focus. The renderer adds one white glow layer; it does not paint a second green shape.

`gilvy qc` on an encode of `examples/ep01-mie-ayam.yaml` reports these ratios at 80% of each scene (Jakarta rounds to 7.8%).
