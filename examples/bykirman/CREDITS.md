# Photo credits for `garam.yaml`

The plates in `plates/` are crops of openly licensed photographs from Wikimedia Commons. Foreground files (`*-fg.webp`) are the same crop with the subject cut out by rembg (`u2net`, CPU) so the shot can parallax. Background files (`*-bg.jpg`) keep the original outside the subject and a blur inside the hole. Collage cards are separate crops. The episode code stays under the repository license.
